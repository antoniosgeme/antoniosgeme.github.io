F(z) = Ue−iα(z−z0) + iΓ/2π · ln(z−z0) + κeiα/(z−z0)

The streamfunction ψ = Im F is plotted directly as contours, and the pressure follows from C_p = 1 − |dF/dζ|²/U². A closed body appears only when the doublet balances the stream: at κ = Ua² the circle of radius a becomes a streamline. Turn on the Joukowski transform to map that circle to an airfoil, then apply the Kutta condition to fix the circulation that makes the flow leave the trailing edge smoothly.
